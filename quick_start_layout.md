# 🚀 QUICK START: Layout Builder

## 3 Simple Steps

### 1️⃣ Click Button
Click **"🎨 Layout Builder"** in toolbar (purple gradient button on the right)

### 2️⃣ Double-Click Canvas
**Double-click anywhere** on the gray grid canvas

### 3️⃣ Choose Content
Pick from the modal:
- **🧪 Plate Schematic**
- **📈 Standard Curve**
- **🧬 Compounds Chart**
- **📊 Results Table**
- **📝 Text Box**
- **📉 Individual Cytokine Plots** (e.g., IL-6, TNF-α, etc.)

---

## That's It! 🎉

Each item appears where you double-clicked.

**Then:**
- **Drag** to move
- **Resize** from corners
- **Delete** with ✕
- **Double-click again** to add more

**When done:**
- Click **💾 Save** to download
- Click **✕** to close

---

## Pro Tips

✅ **Double-click** = Smart selector with all your data
✅ **Right-click** = Quick generic menu (still works)
✅ **Cytokines** = Each one listed individually by name
✅ **Position** = Items appear where you double-clicked
