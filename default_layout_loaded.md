# ✅ DEFAULT LAYOUT AUTO-LOADS EVERYTHING!

## What Changed

The Layout 1 tab now **automatically loads ALL items** when you open it - no clicking, no right-clicking, no adding needed!

## What You'll See

### When You Open Layout 1 Tab:

**Automatically loaded items in a 2x3 grid:**

**Top Row:**
1. **🧪 Plate Schematic** (left) - Green border
2. **📈 Standard Curve** (middle) - Blue border
3. **📉 Cytokine Plot** (right) - Red border

**Bottom Row:**
4. **🧬 Compounds Chart** (left) - Purple border
5. **📊 Results Table** (middle) - Orange border
6. **📝 Text Box** (right) - Gray border

All items appear **automatically within 1 second** of opening the tab!

## How to Use

### Step 1: Open Layout 1 Tab
1. **Refresh browser** (Cmd+Shift+R or Ctrl+Shift+R)
2. **Click "🎨 Layout 1" tab**
3. **Wait 1 second** - items appear automatically!

### Step 2: Manage the Layout
**Everything is already there!** Now you can:

✅ **Drag to move** - Click colored header, drag anywhere
✅ **Resize** - Drag circular handles at 4 corners
✅ **Delete** - Click ✕ button to remove items you don't need
✅ **Add more** - Click sidebar items or right-click canvas
✅ **Save** - Click 💾 button to save your arrangement
✅ **Clear** - Click 🗑️ to remove everything and start over
